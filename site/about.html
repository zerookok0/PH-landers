About Landers Superstore

Landers Superstore is the Philippines' premier membership shopping destination, bringing world-class groceries, appliances, home essentials and a curated selection of global brands closer to Filipino families.

Our Stores

With branches across Metro Manila, Cebu, Naga, Bacolod and Davao, Landers serves members both in-store and online through landers.ph. Orders placed online are picked from your nearest branch and delivered to your door.

The Landers Experience

Members enjoy exclusive food counts, multi-purpose counters, a bakery and deli, an in-house pharmacy, and free delivery thresholds on qualifying orders.

Membership

Landers is a membership store. Apply online, renew in a branch, or use the digital membership card in the Landers app. See Membership for details.
