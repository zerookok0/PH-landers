How to Shop Online

1. Set your delivery location

Use “Enter Your Delivery Location” in the header to confirm that your address is serviceable.

2. Fill your cart

Browse a category, tap ADD TO CART, then adjust quantities in the cart page.

3. Choose a slot

Slots are 2-hour windows. Same-day slots close when the branch capacity is reached.

4. Pay

Pay by credit or debit card. You will receive an order number in the format ORD-YYYYMMDD-XXXX and a confirmation screen.

5. Track

Use Track & Trace any time after checkout.
