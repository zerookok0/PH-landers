Delivery Areas

Landers delivers from the branch nearest to your shipping address. Enter your delivery location at the top of the page to confirm availability and the next available 2-hour slot.

Coverage

  • Metro Manila — all cities, 7 days a week (7am – 9pm slots)
  • Cebu — Cebu City, Mandaue, Lapu-Lapu, Talisay
  • Naga — Naga City and adjacent municipalities
  • Bacolod — Bacolod City and nearby towns
  • Davao — Davao City and suburbs

Delivery Fees

Delivery is free above the qualifying basket amount of your membership tier; below that a flat delivery fee applies, shown before payment.

Pick-up in Store

Prefer to collect? Choose “Pick up in store” at checkout and pick your order from the designated counter.
