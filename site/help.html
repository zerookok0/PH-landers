Help Center

Everything you need to place an order, fix a delivery issue, or use your membership online.

Contact Us

Online Customer Service is available 7am – 9pm, Monday to Sunday (excluding holidays). Use the live chat button in the Landers app, or email the customer service desk listed on your order confirmation.

How to Use a Voucher

Vouchers are applied at checkout: enter the voucher code in the cart summary, confirm the discount appears in the order total, then continue to payment. Vouchers are single-use unless stated otherwise and cannot be combined with other promotions.

How to Save a Credit Card

Saved cards are tokenized by our payment partner and never stored on Landers servers. To keep checkout fast on your next order, tick “Save card for future purchases” when paying.

How to Shop Online

Enter your delivery location, add items to the cart, choose your delivery slot, and pay. Read the full guide in How to Shop Online.

Track an Order

Use Track & Trace with your order number (format ORD-YYYYMMDD-XXXX).
