Landers Membership

Membership unlocks member pricing, exclusive counts, and free delivery thresholds. Apply, renew or look up your benefits below.

Apply Membership

Apply online with a valid government ID and a selfie for verification. Digital cards are issued immediately; physical cards follow by courier.

Renew Membership

Renew at any Landers branch counter or through the app. Renewal keeps your card number and accumulated vouchers.

Membership Benefits

  • Member pricing across the whole catalogue
  • Exclusive “Selection by Landers” merchandise
  • Free delivery above the tier threshold
  • Fuel discounts at partner stations

Digital Membership Card

Your card lives in the Landers app. Show the QR at the counter, or use the card number at checkout to attach member pricing to online orders.

Rewards Hub

Collect stamps and vouchers in the Rewards Hub inside the app. Vouchers can be applied at cart or checkout.

Login

Sign in with your registered email or mobile number. This clone renders the storefront only; member accounts are not created here.
